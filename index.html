03 / The band關於樂團

DIFFERENT ROOTS.不同來處。
SAME DRIVE.同一股衝勁。

Lefthand Drive is a five-piece rock band based in Singapore. Formed in 2023, the band brings together musicians from different international backgrounds through vocals, two guitars, bass and drums. Their released single Paradise and live performances introduce a shared rock sound shaped by five individual perspectives.Lefthand Drive 是一支以新加坡為基地的五人搖滾樂團，成立於 2023 年。來自不同國際背景的團員，以主唱、雙吉他、貝斯與鼓交織出共同的搖滾聲音。已發行單曲〈Paradise〉及現場演出，呈現五位音樂人各自的觀點與默契。
