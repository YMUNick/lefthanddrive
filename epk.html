01 / THE BAND01／關於樂團

SHORT BIO / ENGLISH

Lefthand Drive is a five-piece rock band based in Singapore. Formed in 2023, the band brings together musicians from different international backgrounds through vocals, two guitars, bass and drums. Their released single Paradise and live performances introduce a shared rock sound shaped by five individual perspectives.

短版團介／繁體中文

Lefthand Drive 是一支以新加坡為基地的五人搖滾樂團，成立於 2023 年。來自不同國際背景的團員，以主唱、雙吉他、貝斯與鼓交織出共同的搖滾聲音。已發行單曲〈Paradise〉及現場演出，呈現五位音樂人各自的觀點與默契。

Read the full biography · English / 中文閱讀完整團介・English／中文

Formed in Singapore in 2023, Lefthand Drive brings five musicians with different international backgrounds into one rock band. Nick fronts the group on vocals, with CY and Julian on guitars, Wei Liang on bass and Vijay on drums. The band's identity grows out of that meeting of perspectives: a shared commitment to making music together and bringing it to a live audience. Their released single Paradise offers a starting point for discovering the band. Documented performances include Phil Studio and Venom in Singapore, and Julian’s Friends in Cambodia. Lefthand Drive welcomes conversations with venues, festival programmers and artists about performances and cross-border collaboration.

Lefthand Drive 於 2023 年在新加坡成立，由五位擁有不同國際背景的音樂人組成。Nick 擔任主唱，CY 與 Julian 負責吉他，Wei Liang 擔任貝斯手，Vijay 擔任鼓手。樂團的個性來自不同觀點的相遇，以及一起創作、把音樂帶到現場觀眾面前的共同投入。 已發行單曲〈Paradise〉是認識樂團的起點。現有演出紀錄包括新加坡的 Phil Studio、Venom，以及柬埔寨的 Julian’s Friends。樂團歡迎場館、音樂節策展人及音樂人洽談演出與跨國合作。
